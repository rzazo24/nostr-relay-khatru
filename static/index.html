    Rules and privacy

    • Open for reading and writing, no sign-up. Rate limits apply per IP; if it gets abused it will be tightened.
    • Direct messages are only delivered to their sender and recipient after they authenticate (NIP-42).
    • The relay does not log message content or IP addresses.
    • Notes are public: anyone can read what you publish here, and other relays may hold copies.
